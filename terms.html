Terms of Use

Effective upon BIR accreditation. Sample wording for legal review.

  1. Who these terms cover

    These terms are between Eastland Printink, Inc. ("EPI", "we") and the taxpayer applying for the EPI Digitized Invoice System ("you"). Ticking the agreement box means you accept them. They apply from the day you apply. Before your account goes live you also sign a Client Service Agreement with us. That agreement governs the service, and if it differs from these terms, the agreement prevails.

  2. Applying and validation

    Before accepting you, we check that your business legally exists, including its BIR registration (Form 2303), and that the applicant can act for it (RMC VII.1). As our own policy, we accept only a taxpayer that the BIR requires to issue electronic invoices by the deadline. We answer within 3 working days and may ask for more information. If we decline, we tell you why and charge nothing. Documents from a declined or closed application are deleted after 30 days, and you may apply again. Your account opens only after acceptance. It goes live only when you have signed the Client Service Agreement in ink and had it notarized, we have checked the signed copy, you have paid the safety deposit, the setup checks are done, and our BIR accreditation is in force.

  3. Who acts for your business

    The applicant becomes the account owner. If the applicant is an authorized representative, your business gives us a secretary's certificate, board resolution or special power of attorney that names them and authorizes them to complete onboarding, sign agreements, give us written authorizations and attest to your tax profile. Only the signatory or representative we validated may do those things. Tell us when that authority ends or changes. We accept a new representative only with a new authority document.

  4. Accreditation status

    Our BIR accreditation status is shown under About the service, below. Until accreditation is granted, the system is a demonstration and no live transactions are processed.

  5. What the service does

    We help you issue electronic invoices, send them to the BIR, queue and resend them when the connection is down, and prepare your electronic sales reports.

  6. Your responsibilities

    You are responsible for the accuracy of your invoices, books, returns and tax. You keep your login credentials secure. Your Permit to Issue belongs to you; we may help you apply for it but acquire no interest in it. You choose your tier, and you are responsible for choosing the right one and for declaring your usage truthfully. Our check at onboarding is not a guarantee that the tier fits, and you have no claim against us because the tier you chose proved too high or too low.

  7. Who owns your data

    You own your records. Invoice and sales data sent to the BIR, or held for it, are the BIR's tax-administration records. EPI has no ownership, lien or intellectual-property right over that data or over your Permit to Issue.

  8. How we use your data

    Only to provide invoicing, reporting, storage, support, security, reconciliation and legal compliance. We do not sell, monetize, profile, combine or advertise with your data, and we do not use it for credit scoring or competitive intelligence. Any third party that handles it is disclosed to and approved by the BIR. The approved parties are listed in Settings, on the BIR accreditation tab. Each is bound in writing to the same duties of confidentiality, security, retention and hand-over as EPI. Before we add or change one, we tell the BIR, wait for its approval and tell you. We follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

  9. BIR access

    The BIR may access your invoice data, metadata, audit logs, acknowledgments and rejections as the law allows.

  10. Fees

    Our fees are shown before you apply. You pay a fee for each invoice you issue, set by your tier. Each tier has a monthly allowance. Invoices above the allowance and a 1% margin cost an excess fee, which is the tier's fee plus 25%. You can move to a larger tier at any time, from the first day of the next month, at no charge. A smaller tier is agreed with us at renewal. Every month we check your use. If it is below the range of your tier, we charge that month at the fee of the tier your use falls in and bill the difference. VAT is added to every fee. There is no onboarding fee, no monthly minimum, and no charge for branches, users, connections, exports or leaving. Once an invoice is issued, its fee is not refunded, even if you later cancel the invoice or the buyer's payment is reversed, unless our system caused the error. We give 30 days' notice before any price change. Our fees are billed to your business only. We do not add them to the invoices you issue to your buyers.

  11. Safety deposit

    Before your account goes live you pay a refundable safety deposit, set by your tier: Tier 1, ₱5,000; Tier 2, ₱11,000; Tier 3, ₱51,000; Tier 4, ₱101,000; Tier 5, ₱1,010,000. It is not a fee, a prepayment or a minimum charge, and you cannot use it in place of paying a bill. We hold it without interest. If a bill is not paid by its due date, we may, after written notice, take the unpaid amount, with the interest and penalty, from the deposit, and you restore it within 15 days. If you move to a larger tier, you pay the difference before it starts. When the agreement ends, we refund the deposit within 30 days after your final statement, after setting off anything you still owe.

  12. How the fee is collected

    When a buyer pays an invoice through the System, the payment provider deducts our fee and VAT from that payment, pays it to us and settles the rest to your bank or e-wallet account in real time or on the next banking day. The provider is your own, which you instruct in writing to make the deduction, or our partner. We do not hold your money. The provider's own charge is between the provider and the party that pays it. A fee we cannot deduct, because the buyer did not pay through the System or the payment is smaller than the fee, is billed in your monthly statement. A failed deduction never stops you from issuing. If you cancel your instruction to the provider, we bill your fees monthly, and the cancellation is a breach unless you give a working replacement within 15 days.

  13. Late payment

    Bills are due in 15 days. A late payment carries interest of 1% a month and a penalty of a further 1% a month on the unpaid amount. If you do not pay, we may take legal action to collect, and you pay reasonable attorney's fees and collection costs as the court allows. You can pay by bank transfer, bank deposit, check, card, GCash, Maya or online banking, or set up auto-debit.

  14. Getting your records out

    You can export your records at any time in a machine-readable, non-proprietary format, and have them transferred to you, the BIR or another accredited provider. We do not withhold records because of a dispute, unpaid fees, termination or a change of provider.

  15. Service interruptions

    When transmission is interrupted we queue and retry without losing or duplicating invoices. Manually issued invoices are recorded and linked to their electronic copies. We provide an evidence report of the delay on request.

  16. Security incidents

    We send the BIR a written incident report within 24 hours of discovering a critical incident, and notify you of breaches as the law requires.

  17. Term and when your account ends

    The agreement runs for one year and renews yearly. You can end it at any time on 30 days' notice. We can end it without cause only on 90 days' notice, and either of us can end it for a material breach that is not cured. Your legal duties continue. Our duties to store, protect, keep confidential, give access to and migrate your records continue after the agreement ends, and after our insolvency, a merger or a change of control. We keep your records until the BIR confirms in writing that migration to its systems is complete, and apply legal holds when required. We may stop new invoicing only on a BIR directive, if the BIR suspends or revokes your Permit to Issue, if we reasonably find that your account is used for fraud or threatens the security of the system, or at your request. We do not stop new invoicing, or restrict your records, because of unpaid fees, a short deposit or a failed deduction. We never block your access to existing records.

  18. Our responsibility

    We are liable for fraud, willful misconduct, gross negligence, material breach, unauthorized access, transmission failures caused by our systems or subcontractors, and storage, backup or portability failures. We are not responsible for the payment provider, which acts under a separate agreement between you and the provider. No waiver inconsistent with the BIR circular applies.

  19. Changes

    We give 30 days' notice of changes to these terms.

  20. Governing law and contact

    Philippine law governs. Disputes go to the courts of Quezon City. Contact our compliance officer at compliance@eastlandprintink.ph.

Hosting and processors

Each party below was disclosed to the BIR and approved before it handled any data. A change needs the BIR's approval first, and we tell you.

  • Philippine telecom SMS gateway: sends one-time codes and notices by SMS. Philippines. BIR-DISC-2026-0006.
  • Sentral Pay Services, Inc.: our payment partner, collects the fee for clients who choose it. Philippines. BIR-DISC-2026-0007.
