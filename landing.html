Electronic invoicing under RR 11-2025

Issue, send to the BIR and deliver invoices in one system.

DIS computes VAT, numbers every invoice in sequence, tracks whether it reached the BIR, and sends a copy to your buyer automatically, across all your branches.

What DIS is

Connects your invoices to the BIR

One list of invoices for the head office and every branch, with the daily closings. An issued invoice is never edited. A wrong invoice is cancelled and a new one is issued.

Why electronic invoicing

Controls for common invoicing errors

VAT computation, numbering and approval of corrections are built in.

Automatic VAT & numbering
Every invoice computes VAT and is assigned the next sequence number. No manual computation, no gaps in the numbers.
Cancel and reissue
A wrong invoice is cancelled, with its number retired and the cancelled copy kept in Invoices. The cancellation can need a second person's approval.
Multiple branches
One taxpayer, many branches. Each branch has its own invoice numbers, which can be viewed together or separately.
Role-based access
Owners, approvers and branch staff see only what their role allows.
Buyer copy tracking
Every buyer copy is logged as sent, and failed deliveries are shown.
Audit trail
Every cancellation is recorded against the invoice it withdraws, with who approved it and why.
How it works

How it works

1
Issue
Create the invoice. VAT and the next number are computed.
2
Send to the BIR
The invoice is sent to the BIR. Its status is shown at each stage.
3
Deliver
A copy is sent to the buyer automatically and its delivery is tracked.
4
Correct, if needed
A wrong invoice is cancelled and a new one is issued. The original is never edited.
5
Report
Each period is summarized in a report and a Z reading.
